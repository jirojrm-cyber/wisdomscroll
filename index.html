How to use

Reading
  1. Swipe up for the next idea, down to go back.
  2. Like ideas you enjoy; the feed leans toward them.
  3. Save ideas to keep them in Saved, and group them into collections there.
  4. Share makes an image of the idea, in three styles and sizes.
  5. Tap the topic label on a card to read one topic or a mix.
  6. Tap Discover again to shuffle all your ideas.
  7. ✒ fresh quotes appears when newly written ideas are waiting; tap it to jump there.
  8. The headphones read ideas aloud and move on by themselves.
Get a free Gemini API key

New ideas are written by AI. Google Gemini is free to start and takes about 2 minutes.

  1. Open Google AI Studio with the button below and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Get API key (in the menu ☰ on a phone).
  3. Tap Create API key. If asked, choose or create a project.
  4. Tap Copy next to the new key.
  5. Come back here and open AI & Generation. Choose Google Gemini, paste the key and tap Save key.
  6. Tap Test connection. “Connected to Google Gemini” means it works.
Open Google AI Studio

Google AI Studio is not available in mainland China. There, use DeepSeek or Doubao below.

Other AI providers

DeepSeek: at platform.deepseek.com, create an API key and add a little credit. Choose DeepSeek in AI & Generation and save the key.

Doubao: in the Volcano Engine Ark console, create an API key and activate a Doubao model. Choose Doubao, save the key, and set the model ID if needed.

Claude: at console.anthropic.com, create an API key and add credit.

Good to know

Your key stays on your device and is only sent to its AI provider. The daily limit in AI & Generation keeps usage under control.

Help & Support

Frequently asked questions
Are these real quotations?

No. Each card states an idea from the named book in the AI's own words. The app checks each idea twice, but nothing reads the book itself, so treat a card as a pointer to the book and not as a quotation.

Why do I need an API key?

The ideas are written by an AI service. The app has no server of its own, so it uses a key from your own account. A Google Gemini key is free to create at aistudio.google.com. In mainland China, DeepSeek (platform.deepseek.com) and Doubao (Volcano Engine Ark console) work without a VPN and cost very little. Add the key in AI & Generation.

How do I share the app with a friend?

Tap Share WisdomScroll in Settings and send the link. Your friend opens it, adds it to the Home Screen and adds their own key. Your key, ideas and saved cards are never shared.

What happens to ideas after I swipe past them?

They stay on this device. Swipe back to see them, or tap Discover to shuffle every stored idea into a new order. The app keeps the most recent 300 and remembers older books and ideas so they are not repeated.

How do I read only one topic?

Tap the topic label at the top of any card, or use Reading Preferences, and choose a topic. Choose Mix of my topics to go back.

How do I use DeepSeek or Doubao?

DeepSeek: create a key at platform.deepseek.com under API keys, add a little credit, then choose DeepSeek in AI & Generation and save the key. Doubao: in the Volcano Engine Ark console, create an API key and open (activate) a Doubao model, then choose Doubao in AI & Generation, save the key, and pick the model, or choose Other model ID and type the model ID or your endpoint ID (ep-…). Tap Test connection to check.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for the ideas already on the device. Writing new ideas, loading covers and syncing need a connection.

How do I use it on another device?

Open the same address there and add it to the Home Screen. To carry your saved ideas and keys across, create an account in Settings, Account, and sign in on each device.

I forgot my password

In Settings, Account, type your email and tap Forgot password. Open the link in the email on the same device and choose a new password. Your saved ideas are kept; add your API key again afterwards. Accounts made with a username cannot be reset by email: contact the developer through Contact us.

If new ideas stop arriving
  1. Open AI & Generation and read the status line under Generation.
  2. Tap Test connection. A rejected key means it was pasted wrongly, has expired, or belongs to the other provider.
  3. A message about credit, billing or quota means the provider account needs credit. Add it with the provider, or switch provider.
  4. "Could not reach" means this network cannot reach the provider. Try another network.
  5. Check that today's count is below the daily limit, and that automatic writing is on under Background refill.
Exporting your data
  1. Open Data & Storage.
  2. Tap Export all data to save a backup file. API keys are left out unless you switch that option on.
  3. To move to a new device without sync, open Data & Storage there and choose Restore from a backup file.

Privacy Policy

A plain-language description of how this app handles information. It is not legal advice.

What the app keeps

WisdomScroll stores your feed, saved and liked ideas, preferences, display name, API keys and sync sign-in state in your browser's local storage on your own device. The developer of the app does not receive this information and runs no server that collects it.

What leaves your device

AI providers. When ideas are written, a request goes directly from your device to the provider you selected, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek or Volcano Engine (ByteDance), authenticated with your API key. It contains the writing instructions, your chosen topics and language, and lists of the books, authors and ideas already shown to you. Each provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy.

Open Library. Book titles and author names are sent to Open Library, run by the Internet Archive, to find covers and to confirm that a book exists.

Google Fonts. The app's typefaces are loaded from Google's font service.

Optional account. If you create an account, the sync service (Supabase) stores your email address, a protected form of your password, and one record with your saved and liked ideas, collections and preferences. Database rules let only your signed-in account read or change that record. Your API keys are encrypted on your device with a key made from your password before they are stored, so they cannot be read by the service or by the developer. Your email address is used only to sign in and to send password-reset links you ask for.

Spotify. The music button only opens the playlist link you chose. The app does not connect to your Spotify account.

Hosting. The app's files are served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, can log requests for those files.

What the app does not do

It shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share information.

Your choices

You can export everything, remove your API keys, sign out of sync, or clear all app data at any time in Settings. Clearing your browser's site data has the same effect as clearing all app data.
